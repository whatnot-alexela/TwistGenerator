## The menu

Five entries, replacing the current two.

| # | Entry | Costs | Built from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Почитать примеры твистов | free | the catalogue, no API call |
| 2 | Случайный твист | 1 twist | new |
| 3 | Твист по описанию ситуации | 1 twist | new — mode 2 without the compatibility map |
| 4 | Твист по ситуации с рекомендацией Откровения | 2 twists | today's «начать с описания ситуации» |
| 5 | Твист по формуле | 2 twists | today's «собрать по формуле» |

Entries 4 and 5 are the deliberate path: the user chooses the mechanics and
gets the optional parameters. Entries 2 and 3 are the quick path.

### 1. Reading examples — free

The catalogue holds 24 formulas, 29 worked examples and 20 film or book
references, all from appendices `1е` and `6и`. Reading them costs nothing: the
text is already written, and the mechanics — which causes were swapped, which
paradox results — is computed from the rule, not generated.

This is the one entry that makes the bot useful to somebody who has spent
nothing and may never spend anything. It is also the cheapest way to teach the
methodology before a user pays for a generation.

### 2. A random twist — 1 twist

A formula drawn at random, generated, and named. Cheapest generation the bot
offers, and the least commitment to ask of a newcomer.

### 3. From a described situation — 1 twist

The user describes the Expectation; the bot reads it into the left half of a
formula, picks the Revelation cause itself, and generates. No buttons, no map:
one message in, one twist out.

### 4. From a situation, with a recommended Revelation — 2 twists

Today's mode 2, with the second-cause step shortened (see question 4).

### 5. By formula — 2 twists

Today's mode 1, unchanged.

## One twist or two

Today every generation returns exactly two twists — the output contract says
so, and it is frozen because the author wrote it. Entries 2 and 3 need a
second contract that asks for one.

What that saves, from the single measured generation: input is fixed at about
21 400 tokens ($0.107) and is paid whatever the answer's length; output was
5 700 tokens ($0.143) for two twists. So one twist costs roughly **$0.18**
against **$0.25** for two — cheaper, but not half, because the methodology in
the prompt is paid either way.

## «Как это работает»

Four additions to `/help`:

- **What a twist is** — Expectation and Revelation, in one line each, before
  anything about formulas.
- **The two paradoxes**, briefly: №1 is a cause foreign to the kind of change
  in the Expectation, №2 is one in the Revelation. Two sentences and an example
  each, no theory.
- **Every entry follows the methodology**, the ready-made examples included.
- **Which model writes the twists** — Claude Opus 5, by Anthropic.

## Open questions

1. **Quota.** Is «1 twist» / «2 twists» the price in quota units — one unit and
   two? And how many free units a day, all entries sharing one allowance?
   Today it is 3 by formula plus 1 by situation, counted separately.

2. **The examples (entry 1).** The mechanics under each example: written from
   the rule — formula, causes, paradox, in the bot's own words, costing
   nothing — or asked of the model, which makes a free entry cost $0.18 a
   press? And how does a reader move through them: a random one each press, or
   chosen by type of change?

3. **The random twist (entry 2).** Drawn from all 192 formulas, or only from
   the 24 that have a worked example? Shown before generating, or revealed
   with the twist?

4. **«С рекомендацией Откровения» (entry 4).** Three readings of this, and they
   are different pieces of work:
   - the bot picks one Revelation cause and offers it, with the other three
     behind a «выбрать другую» button;
   - the four buttons stay but their labels get shorter;
   - the model is asked which Revelation suits this situation best — an extra
     call, so an extra cost.

   Which one?

5. **Extra parameters for entries 4 and 5.** Today: genre, characters, setting.
   Add anything — era, tone, length, audience? Each one is another question
   between the user and their twist, so they earn their place or they don't.

6. **Where the model is named.** In `/help` only, or under every twist as well?

7. **The old commands.** `/formula` and `/situation` now point at entries 5 and
   4. Keep them?

8. **Consent** (backlog item 3) is still waiting on the operator's name and a
   contact address. It blocks nothing here, but it does block opening the bot
   to strangers.
